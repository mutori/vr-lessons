Heartbeat: The Heart and Circulation Form 2

On a Meta Quest: press ENTER VR at the bottom of the page. Hands work best; controllers work the same way.

  • Pinch a label to pick it up, then pinch at a glowing dot on the heart to place it (or point at the dot and pinch).
  • To choose a part of the heart, touch it and pinch, or point at it and pinch.
  • Choose a level on the panel to your left.

On a computer: click labels, dots and parts, drag to look around, R to reset the view.
