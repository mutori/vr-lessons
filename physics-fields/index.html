Field Lab (A-Level Physics, Form 6)

On a Meta Quest: press ENTER VR at the bottom of the page. Hands work best; controllers work the same way.

  • Touch panel buttons with your fingertip (or the controller tip).
  • Pinch a yellow slider knob or the bar magnet and slide it, or use the − and + buttons.
  • For things out of reach, point and pinch (or pull the trigger).
  • Right thumbstick turns you. You do not need to walk.

On a computer: click buttons, drag a slider knob or the magnet, drag the background to look around, scroll to zoom.
