Circuit Workshop: Electricity Form 4

On a Meta Quest: press ENTER VR at the bottom of the page. Hands work best; controllers work the same way.

  • Pinch a part on the tray to pick it up, then pinch over a glowing gap on the circuit board to put it in.
  • Pinch a switch on the board to open or close it. Pinch any other part on the board to take it out again.
  • Touch buttons with a fingertip. Choose a level on the panel to your left.

On a computer: click parts, gaps and buttons, drag to look around, R to reset the view.
