O-Level Science Lab VR

On a Meta Quest: press ENTER VR at the bottom of the page. Hands work best; controllers work the same way.

  • Touch buttons with your fingertip (or the controller tip).
  • Pinch a tool to pick it up. It stays in your hand. Bring it to its target and pinch again to use it. Pinch in empty air to put it back.
  • For things out of reach, point and pinch (or pull the trigger).
  • Move between benches with the Prev / Start / Next buttons on each bench, or a green floor pad. Thumbstick walking is off unless you switch it on at the start table.

On a computer: click to use, drag to look, WASD to walk, 1–5 jump to a bench, H back to the start.
