O-Level Science Lab VR

On a Meta Quest: press ENTER VR at the bottom of the page.

  • Point the laser and pull the trigger (or pinch with hand tracking) to use things.
  • Point at a green floor pad and pull the trigger to move to that bench.
  • Right thumbstick: turn. Left thumbstick: walk.

On a computer: click to use, drag to look, WASD to walk, 1–5 jump to a bench, H back to the start.
