Orbit Command (A-Level Physics, Form 5)

On a Meta Quest: press ENTER VR at the bottom of the page. Hands work best; controllers work the same way.

  • Touch console buttons with your fingertip (or the controller tip).
  • Pinch things that glow when your hand is near (the throttle lever, the probe, the satellite, switches) to grab and move them. The − and + buttons give fine steps.
  • For things out of reach, point and pinch (or pull the trigger).
  • A bouncing arrow shows what to try next. Right thumbstick turns you.

On a computer: click buttons, drag levers, knobs and glowing objects, drag the background to look around, scroll to zoom.
