Atom Forge: Atoms, Ions and Bonds Form 3

On a Meta Quest: press ENTER VR at the bottom of the page. Hands work best; controllers work the same way.

  • Touch a button with your fingertip, or point at it and pinch (trigger).
  • To add an electron, pinch on a shell ring (or point at it and pinch).
  • In the bonding level, pinch an outer electron to pick it up, then pinch on another atom’s outer shell to give it away.
  • Choose a level on the panel to your left.

On a computer: click buttons, shells and electrons, drag to look around, R to reset the view.
