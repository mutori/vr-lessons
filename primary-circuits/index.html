Light Up the Village (Grades 5–7)

Help Tendai's village build its own circuits: six story chapters with levels, a night-time party, and a free-build workshop. On a Meta Quest: press ENTER VR at the bottom of the page. Hands work best; controllers work the same way.

  • Pinch a part on the tray to pick it up. Bring it over an empty gap on the board and pinch again to place it. Pinch in the air to put it back.
  • Touch the red switch lever and the buttons with your fingertip.
  • For things out of reach, point and pinch (or pull the trigger).
  • The buttons on the front of the table choose a chapter (W is the workshop), read the board aloud, restart, clear the board and go to the next chapter.
  • In chapter 6 and the workshop, a quick pinch on a placed cell turns it round.

On a computer: click a part, then click an empty gap. Click a switch lever to flip it. In chapter 6 and the workshop, right-click a cell to turn it round. Drag to look around. Keys: 1–7 chapters (7 is the workshop), R restart, C clear, N next.
